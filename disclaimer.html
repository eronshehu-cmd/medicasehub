Medical disclaimer

Everything on this site is published for education and information only. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Do not use this for self-diagnosis

The cases here describe specific situations with context that does not repeat. Similar symptoms can have entirely different causes, and the outcome in one case says nothing about the outcome in another. For any health concern, see a licensed clinician.

In an emergency

If you have an urgent medical problem, contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest hospital. Do not spend time searching online first.

Sources and anonymisation

Cases come from experiences shared publicly and from sources that permit reuse. All identifying details are removed before publication: names, locations, exact dates, and distinguishing features. If you recognise a case as your own and want it taken down, write to us through the contact page and we will remove it without asking for an explanation.

Accuracy

We aim to be accurate, but medicine changes and errors are possible. The content is provided as is, without warranty. MedicaseHub accepts no liability for decisions made on the basis of it.

Clinical imagery

Some cases include operative images. These are always hidden behind a warning and appear only if the reader clicks to open them.

No relationship is created

Reading this site, or writing to us, does not create a clinician-patient relationship. We cannot answer questions about your own condition and will not attempt to.
