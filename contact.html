Requesting a takedown

Write with the subject line "Takedown" and the page link. We do not ask for an explanation and we do not ask you to prove anything. The case comes down.

Submitting a case

Include the timeline and the outcome. Do not send anything that identifies the patient: names, exact dates, locations, or images with distinguishing features. If you are the patient, say so, and tell us how much detail you are comfortable publishing.

Corrections

If something on the site is wrong, point to the page and tell us what it should say. We correct it and note the change at the bottom of the page.
