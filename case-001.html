Case MC-001 · General surgery

Abdominal pain that changed course in 24 hours

This case shows how much weight the timeline carries, not just the first clinical picture.

Specialty
General surgery
Procedure
Diagnostic laparoscopy
Length of stay
48 hours
Outcome
Full recovery

Presentation

The patient presented with pain in the lower right abdomen, a low-grade fever, and loss of appetite. The initial examination showed nothing alarming, and the first working assessment leaned toward a non-surgical cause.

How it evolved

Within 24 hours the pain intensified and localised more sharply. Repeat bloods showed rising inflammatory markers, which moved the assessment away from the original impression.

The intervention

The team proceeded to diagnostic laparoscopy. The findings confirmed the clinical suspicion and were treated in the same operative session, with no need to convert to open surgery.

Outcome

The patient was discharged after 48 hours with an uncomplicated recovery. A follow-up review two weeks later showed no complications.

What this case teaches

A single normal examination does not rule out the diagnosis. A planned reassessment within 12 to 24 hours is often what changes the decision.
